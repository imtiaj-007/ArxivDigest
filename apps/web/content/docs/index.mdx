ArxivDigest is an autonomous daily AI agent that scans new arxiv `cs.AI`, `cs.LG`,
and `cs.CL` submissions, produces structured TL;DRs, classifies them by theme, and
ranks them by novelty and impact — all on free-tier infrastructure, $0/month.

## Reading the digest

Each paper card shows:

- <Eye className="inline size-4 text-fd-primary" /> **Theme badges** — 1–3
  taxonomy slugs, most relevant first (see [taxonomy](/docs/themes)).
- <Sparkles className="inline size-4 text-fd-primary" /> **Impact score** —
  blended novelty (cosine distance to neighbors in embedding space) +
  LLM-judged impact, on a 0–1 scale. Most papers land in `0.4–0.6`;
  see [methodology → rank](/docs/methodology#rank).
- <FileCode className="inline size-4 text-fd-primary" /> **Structured TL;DR**
  — Problem / Approach / Result / Why it matters. Grounded strictly in the
  abstract; the prompt forbids inventing claims.
